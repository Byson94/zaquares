---
title: The Failing Water Quality/Pollution Index
description: Understanding fundamental limitations with water quality/pollution index models.
date: 2026-09-23
---

After rigorous research, we realized that a WQI/WPI Index cannot truly be general purpose, as in working the same way, equally, in the entire world. Each region has its own natural composition of chemical and physical parameters that may be too high for another region. Beacuse of this reason, a fully general purpose monolithic WQI/WPI Index may identify pollution in a region that is in its natural, healthy form.

For this reason, we will be making our WPI definition calibrated to just Kerala, so that it works reliably in our locality. If we were
to calibrate it to the world standards, then we might be making a WPI definition that is flawed from the start for our locality specifically.
This way, we hope that our WPI Definition serves the use for which it was made.

Regarding the progress on the WPI Definition, We are still working on the full specifications. Especially the general body of the parameter conversion equations that will dicatate the nonlinear nature of our WPI Definition. Another document on the specifications of the WPI will be published soon.
